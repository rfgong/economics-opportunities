# Weekly Economics Research-Presentation and Recruiting/Outreach Opportunity Scan

Prepare my weekly economics research-presentation and recruiting/outreach opportunity scan.

I am a Yale Economics PhD student focused on Political Economy, Industrial Organization, and Environmental Economics. Optimize for intellectual and career expected value, not completeness.

## CACHE

Read these public GitHub files first:

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rfgong/economics-opportunities/main/venue_calendar.json
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rfgong/economics-opportunities/main/recruiting_calendar.json
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rfgong/economics-opportunities/main/state.json

Treat the calendars as durable monitoring priors, not as proof of a current open call. Treat `state.json` page changes and failures only as monitoring signals.

## RESEARCH PRESENTATION OBJECTIVE

Prioritize opportunities that would put my work in front of:

(a) plausible journal editors/referees,  
(b) plausible future external tenure-letter writers or senior field leaders,  
(c) highly productive potential future coauthors.

Recent junior economists' presentation histories are useful primarily for discovering where strong early-career economists actually present. Senior editors/field leaders and relevant organizers are primarily an audience-value signal.

## VENUE CLASSES

Classify research opportunities as:

- **GRADUATE:** specifically designed for PhD students / junior researchers.
- **PROFESSIONAL:** ordinary professional workshops/conferences with a genuine open route for PhD students.
- **WILDCARD:** industry, government, regulatory, interdisciplinary, or policy forums with exceptional research fit.

Do not impose category quotas.

## WEEKLY PROCESS

1. Inspect tracked venues whose historical opening/deadline windows are relevant now, giving extra attention to pages that changed since the last cache refresh or are flagged for review.
2. Run a novelty sweep for relevant calls not already in the calendar. At minimum check current NBER calls, CEPR open calls, AERE, Industrial Organization Society/IIOC, SITE, FTC Bureau of Economics, and other high-value PE/IO/environmental economics venues surfaced by current research.
3. Verify every surfaced opportunity from an authoritative current-cycle source. Never infer a deadline from a historical cycle.
4. If a tracked venue is more than roughly 60 days past its historical deadline window and no current-cycle call or closure can be verified, flag it as **NEEDS REVIEW** rather than assuming "not yet posted."
5. Do not recommend invitation-only events unless there is a genuine public application, submission, or nomination route.
6. Return fewer items rather than padding.

## RESEARCH RANKING

Rank principally on:

- relevant editor/referee audience;
- plausible senior letter-writer exposure;
- productive potential collaborators;
- fit with my current research in PE / IO / environment / regulation / firms;
- organizer overlap with relevant active researchers;
- realistic PhD eligibility and paper-stage fit;
- acceptance feasibility;
- quality of discussion format and funding/logistics.

## RECRUITING & INDUSTRY OUTREACH

Return at most 2 and never let recruiting displace a research-presentation item.

**AMAZON IS PRIORITY 0.**

Every run, search Amazon Economics, Amazon Science, Amazon economist/PhD recruiting, economics PhD informational sessions, and research-oriented graduate programs before other employers.

Then consider Analysis Group / Ignite@AG, leading economic-consulting firms, closely related tech-economics employers, environmental/policy research organizations, and exceptional lower-fit wildcards.

Do **not** fill this section with ordinary job listings or generic internships. A qualifying item should normally provide something beyond submitting an application, such as:

- direct access to economists/research leaders;
- externship or immersive experience;
- research presentation/poster component;
- structured networking or mentorship;
- exposure to economist research projects or career paths;
- unusually strong research-oriented internship programming.

If no recruiting item clears a high bar, state:

> No high-value recruiting/outreach opportunities currently open.

## QUALITY CONTROL

- Use official current-cycle sources for every recommended item.
- Search can discover opportunities, but do not rely on aggregators as sole evidence.
- Do not report expired calls.
- Do not duplicate one event across sections.
- If the GitHub cache is stale or partially failed, continue the live scan but explicitly note any resulting coverage limitation.

## OUTPUT STYLE

Be highly concise. Optimize the research process for completeness, but optimize the report for decision usefulness.

Target **500–700 words total**. Fewer is better when there are few strong opportunities.

### TOP PRESENTATION OPPORTUNITIES

Return at most 3, ranked.

For each:

**Event — CATEGORY**  
Deadline: [exact date] | Presentation: [exact date(s)]  
Application: [brief requirements]  
Official link: [link]

Then at most 2 bullets, each no more than 25 words:

- fit / audience / expected value;
- eligibility, paper stage, funding, or unusual format detail.

Do not restate information already given in the header.

### PRESENTATION ALTERNATES

Return at most 3. One line each:

**Event — CATEGORY** — Deadline [date] | Event [date] — [≤25-word reason it is worth considering but ranks below the top list].

### ON THE HORIZON

Return at most 3. One line each:

**Event** — [verified future opening date OR “historically expected around ___; not yet verified”] — [≤20-word reason to monitor].

### RECRUITING & INDUSTRY OUTREACH OUTPUT

Return at most 2.

For each:

**Program — Organization**  
Deadline: [exact date / rolling] | Program dates: [dates]  
Application: [brief requirements]  
Official link: [link]

Then at most 2 bullets, each no more than 20 words:

- substantive economist/research exposure;
- eligibility, funding, selection, or distinctive feature.

If none clears the bar, state only:

> No high-value recruiting/outreach opportunities currently open.

### QUALITY CONTROL NOTE

Include only if there is a material coverage limitation, unresolved tracked venue, or stale/failed cache that could affect recommendations.

Maximum one sentence.

Do not include:

- a concluding summary of opportunities already ranked;
- descriptions of expired calls unless needed to explain a monitoring gap;
- narrative accounts of the search process;
- repeated audience/fit explanations;
- generic praise of conferences or programs.

When in doubt, omit detail rather than lengthening the report.
